Our Business Verticals

Pontoon Piling

SOGC INFRA provides specialized Pontoon Piling services for projects located in rivers, water bodies, and other challenging aquatic environments. With experienced technical teams and suitable piling equipment, SOGC INFRA is capable of executing pile foundations from floating platforms and pontoons. This service is suitable for river bridges, bridge piers, water intake structures, jetties, and other infrastructure projects where foundation construction is required in or near water.

Sheet Piles

SOGC INFRA provides Sheet Piling solutions for soil retention, excavation support, water control, and foundation protection. Sheet piles are widely used in deep excavations, cofferdams, retaining structures, bridge foundations, riverbank protection, and waterfront construction. SOGC INFRA delivers reliable sheet piling solutions based on project requirements and site conditions, helping ensure stability and safety during construction.

Micro Piles

SOGC INFRA offers Micro Piling services for projects where conventional piling methods may not be suitable due to restricted access, limited working space, low headroom, existing structures, or challenging soil conditions. Micro piles are commonly used for foundation strengthening, structural rehabilitation, bridge foundations, slope stabilization, railway and highway projects, and the strengthening of existing buildings and structures.

RCC Bored Cast-in-Situ Piles

SOGC INFRA specializes in the execution of RCC Bored Cast-in-Situ Pile Foundations for infrastructure, industrial, commercial, and residential projects. The work involves boring to the required depth, installation of reinforcement cages, and concreting to create strong and durable foundations. With extensive experience and modern piling equipment, SOGC INFRA executes bored cast-in-situ piles for bridges, metro projects, expressways, industrial structures, power plants, commercial buildings, and other major infrastructure developments.

Secant(Touching) Piles

SOGC INFRA provides Secant Pile and Touching Pile solutions for projects requiring excavation support, earth retention, and groundwater control. Secant piles are constructed with overlapping piles to create a continuous structural wall, while touching piles are installed closely together to provide effective soil retention. These systems are commonly used for deep basements, underground structures, metro projects, retaining walls, and complex excavation works where ground stability is critical.

Under-Reamed Piles

SOGC INFRA undertakes Under-Reamed Pile Foundation work, particularly for structures constructed on expansive soils, including black cotton soil. Under-reamed piles have enlarged bulbs at specific depths, improving load-bearing capacity and providing better resistance against soil expansion and shrinkage. These foundations are suitable for residential buildings, industrial structures, and other projects located in challenging soil conditions.

Structures

SOGC INFRA provides complete Open Foundation and Infrastructure Structure construction services for highways, railways, bridges, and other civil infrastructure projects. Our scope includes excavation, PCC, reinforcement, shuttering, concreting, and structural construction. SOGC INFRA executes structures such as Minor Bridges (MNB), Vehicular Underpasses (VUP), Box Culverts, Culverts, Retaining Structures, and other RCC structures, ensuring execution in accordance with approved drawings, specifications, and project requirements.

Girder Work

SOGC INFRA provides comprehensive support for Girder Work associated with bridges, flyovers, ROBs, and other infrastructure projects. Our capabilities include RCC structural work, girder casting support, precast structural work, girder erection, and other bridge superstructure activities. With experienced teams and a strong focus on safety and quality, SOGC INFRA supports the efficient execution of critical structural components.

Pile Pier Work

SOGC INFRA provides complete Pile Foundation and Pier Construction services for bridges, flyovers, highways, and infrastructure projects. The scope of work includes pile foundations, pile caps, pier construction, pier caps, reinforcement, shuttering, and RCC concreting work. With strong experience in foundation and structural construction, SOGC INFRA delivers reliable pile and pier solutions for complex infrastructure projects.

Real Estate & Land Development

SOGC INFRA has also expanded into the Real Estate sector, offering services related to land purchase and sale. We assist clients in identifying suitable residential, commercial, and investment land opportunities while facilitating professional coordination between buyers and sellers. With a focus on transparency and long-term value, SOGC INFRA aims to provide reliable support for land and property-related opportunities.

Water Supply & Pipeline

SOGC INFRA provides comprehensive Water Pipeline Construction services for urban, rural, industrial, and infrastructure projects. Our capabilities include pipeline excavation and trenching, pipe laying, jointing, construction of valve chambers, thrust blocks, testing, commissioning, and restoration work. SOGC INFRA works with different types of pipelines, including MS, DI, and HDPE pipelines, delivering reliable solutions for water supply and distribution infrastructure.

Our Equipment & Capabilities

Hydraulic Rigs

SOGC INFRA operates Hydraulic Piling Rigs for the execution of heavy-duty and large-diameter pile foundations. Our hydraulic rigs are capable of working with pile diameters ranging from 450 mm to 1500 mm and can achieve depths of 60+ metres, depending on project and soil conditions. These rigs are suitable for working in various soil strata, including clay, sand, and rock, making them ideal for bridges, metro projects, industrial structures, high-rise buildings, and major infrastructure projects.

Winch/DMC Machine

SOGC INFRA uses Winch and DMC Machines for specialized piling operations, particularly in challenging ground and water conditions. These machines are capable of executing piles with diameters ranging from 600 mm to 1500 mm and depths of 30+ metres. Our Winch/DMC machines are particularly specialized for working in rocky strata and Pontoon Piling, making them suitable for river bridges, bridge piers, and other water-based foundation projects.

Hydraulic DTH

SOGC INFRA operates Hydraulic DTH (Down-the-Hole) Machines for drilling and piling work in hard rock conditions. These machines are capable of working with diameters ranging from 40 mm to 600 mm and depths of 25+ metres. Hydraulic DTH machines are particularly effective in hard rock strata, where conventional boring methods may not be suitable, ensuring efficient and reliable drilling performance.

TMR (Truck Mounted Rotary)

SOGC INFRA operates Truck Mounted Rotary (TMR) Piling Machines for efficient and mobile pile foundation work. These machines are capable of executing piles ranging from 400 mm to 1500 mm in diameter and achieving depths of 40+ metres. TMR machines are particularly suitable for clay and sandy soil strata and provide a fast and efficient solution for projects requiring mobility and high productivity.

Manual Piles

SOGC INFRA also provides Manual Piling services for projects with limited access or locations where heavy machinery cannot be deployed. Using auger piling methods and skilled manpower, we can execute piles up to 450 mm in diameter and approximately 15 metres in depth. Manual piling is a practical solution for small-scale projects, restricted working areas, and locations requiring flexible execution methods.

Tractor Mounted Rotary

SOGC INFRA operates Tractor Mounted Rotary Piling Machines for flexible and cost-effective piling operations. These machines can execute piles up to 600 mm in diameter and approximately 20 metres in depth. They are particularly suitable for clay and sandy soil conditions and are widely used for projects requiring easy mobility and efficient execution in accessible locations.

Pile Testing

SOGC INFRA provides comprehensive Pile Testing services to evaluate the strength, performance, and integrity of pile foundations. Our testing capabilities include Load Tests, Initial Tests, Vertical Load Tests, Pull-Out Tests, Lateral Load Tests, and Pile Integrity Tests. These tests help verify the pile's load-carrying capacity and structural integrity, ensuring compliance with project specifications and engineering requirements.

Man Power

SOGC INFRA provides experienced and reliable construction manpower services for piling, civil, structural and infrastructure projects. Our workforce includes Piling Labour, Steel and Reinforcement Labour, Casting Labour, Structural Labour, Masons, Unskilled Labour, Road Marking Teams, Carpenters, Tile Workers, and other skilled construction professionals. With trained teams available for different project requirements, SOGC INFRA supports clients with quality manpower and efficient execution across various construction activities.
